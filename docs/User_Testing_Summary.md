# CSSE375 User Testing Report

## Instructions Given to Testers
- Create an account.
- Look up and add 2 movies to your watchlist.
- Look up and rate and review 2 movies.
- Look up and follow the user "Peterfrach".
- Change your privacy settings.
- Log out and log back in.
- Remove a movie from your watchlist.
